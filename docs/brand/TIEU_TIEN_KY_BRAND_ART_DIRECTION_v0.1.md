# TIỂU TIÊN KÝ — BRAND / ART DIRECTION v0.1

## Working title
**TIỂU TIÊN KÝ**

Status: Working title. Not trademark-cleared.

## Core visual thesis
**Chibi Cultivation Adventure — Cute Eastern Fantasy**

## Emotional ingredients
- whimsical chibi adventure;
- tu tiên từ phàm nhân → đại năng;
- cơ duyên / pháp bảo / linh thú;
- playful system-driven chaos;
- bright mobile readability;
- expressive characters.

## Signature contrast
**Cute character + spectacular cultivation power.**

## Character baseline
- 2.5–3 heads tall;
- expressive face;
- simplified costume;
- oversized readable weapon/prop;
- transformation grows around the body through aura, floating swords, spell circles, spirits and elemental effects.

## Map baseline
- bright handcrafted fantasy playground;
- floating islands, bamboo, clouds, ponds, villages, spirit creatures;
- small interactive toys/hazards;
- readable at phone scale.

## Tone
> Thế giới tu tiên nguy hiểm, nhưng nhìn vào lại đáng yêu.

## Anti-copy
Do not reproduce any specific character, silhouette, UI, map, monster, lore, animation, sound, icon or distinctive expression from MapleStory, Vân Kiếp, Phàm Nhân Tu Tiên, or any other reference IP.

## Working tagline
**Mỗi trận, một kỳ duyên.**
