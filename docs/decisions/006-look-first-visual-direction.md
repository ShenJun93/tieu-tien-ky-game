## Question

In what order does TTK get to a market-looking build, and what do its characters and arenas look like?

## Context

- After R0.1–R0.3, the Director installed the URP build and saw the same primitives and grey square buttons: "still a demo prototype".
- The code audit (`docs/research/2026-09-unity-ecosystem/04-ttk-code-audit.md`) shows why. Characters, arena, HUD, animation clips and audio are all generated by C# editor scripts, and VFX are created per hit at runtime. No authored art exists.
- The team had no measured visual reference. `05-visual-reference-teardown.md` now measures five shipped games and sets numeric targets.
- The owned 3D characters (BoZo Stylized Modular Characters) have chunky proportions.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the R0 order: combat skeleton, then tooling, then store readiness, and art at G2. **Rejected:** months more of primitives, which is the loop the Director wants to leave.
2. **Look first.** One arena with a BoZo hero and enemies, toon shading, real VFX and HUD, judged against the teardown spec. **Chosen.**

## Decision

1. The next player-facing slice is **"30 seconds that look like a real game"**. R0.4 (combat skeleton), R0.5 (agent tooling) and R0.6 (store readiness) follow it, or run beside it when they do not touch the same files.
2. ~~**Characters use BoZo's chunky proportions.**~~ *Superseded by ADR 008: comedic chibi at about 2.5 heads; BoZo was never owned.* Silhouettes must read at about 10% of screen height.
3. **Arenas use a dark jade stone floor** (the Hades / Warm Snow approach). Characters and VFX carry brightness through rim light, outline and emissive effects.
4. Visual targets and grey-box tells come from `05-visual-reference-teardown.md`. The Director judges the result on a phone, side by side with store screenshots of the reference games.

## Consequences

- Before the slice, delete the procedural content generators, or stop running them, so that authored assets are not overwritten.
- Content whose licence forbids redistribution (Asset Store, Mixamo) lives in a git-ignored local folder. Clones and CI see missing references, so a CI screenshot cannot judge the look.
- `docs/GAME.md` "Look and feel" is updated to match.

## Assumptions

- BoZo plus free CC0 kits and ChatGPT-made 2D art can reach the target look without paid assets.
- Chunky proportions do not hurt the xianxia fantasy when combined with spectacular VFX.

## Review triggers

- The Director's phone check of the look slice fails twice.
- BoZo cannot be animated well enough with Mixamo or Quaternius animations.
