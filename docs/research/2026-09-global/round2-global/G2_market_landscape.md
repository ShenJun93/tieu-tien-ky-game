# G2 — Market Landscape: Mobile Action & Xianxia (2023–2026)

Prepared for: Tiểu Tiên Ký (TTK). Unity, mobile-first, real-time direct-control xianxia action, solo developer plus AI agents, based in Vietnam.
Research date: 2026-09-26. Method: web search and fetch only. No project repository was read.
Source tags: `[lang | P=primary / S=secondary | date]`. "P" means the data owner, the regulator, or the developer/publisher itself. "S" means press, aggregators or wikis reporting third-party estimates (Sensor Tower, AppMagic and similar). Revenue figures are gross player spending estimates unless noted. Quotes are kept under 15 words.

---

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Big-budget anime action games are not a reachable market for TTK.** Wuthering Waves, Zenless Zone Zero and Solo Leveling: Arise earn $100M–$440M in their first year. They are built by teams of hundreds and depend on gacha plus a steady flow of new content. Solo Leveling: Arise shows how fast revenue drops once that content flow slows.
2. **Small teams do win in two lanes:**
   - **Action roguelites.** Premium or light F2P: Soul Knight, Warm Snow, Pascal's Wager, Grimvalor.
   - **Survivor-likes and hybrid-casual games.** Brotato, 20 Minutes Till Dawn, Vampire Survivors mobile, Magic Survival, and the Archero template.
   Xianxia is proven as a **theme and progression fantasy** (cultivation realms, sects, idle growth). It is not proven as a **direct-control action** genre on mobile.
3. **Nobody clearly owns the "xianxia action roguelite with cultivation meta-progression" niche on mobile.** Small Steam titles (e.g. Cultivation Story: Reincarnation) and upcoming "Wuxia & Xianxia Survivors" clones show both interest and early crowding.
4. **For a solo developer in Vietnam, avoid gacha.** Rules on probability disclosure (Korea), game licensing (Vietnam's Decree 147, China's ISBN/版号) and virtual-currency pricing (EU) add a lot of compliance work. Premium, premium-unlock, or ads plus a small IAP layer are the realistic models.

| 小妖问道 (寻道大千 overseas) | Mobile app | >¥250M cumulative on App Store + Google Play; hybrid IAA+IAP | [S36-CN] Sina, Apr 2024 |

| 燕云十六声 | AAA wuxia | See §1a | — |

Not verified this session: 了不起的修仙模拟器 and 觅长生 sales figures.

**Reading.** Chinese audiences pay heavily for cultivation as **numbers going up**: realm breakthroughs, idle accumulation, sect management. The biggest hits are idle or hybrid IAA+IAP games, not skill-based action. TapTap shows small-team "修仙 + 肉鸽 + 割草" (xianxia + roguelite + mowing-down-hordes) titles in 2025 (e.g. 卡包修仙, rated 8.3) [S40-CN]. That points to an emerging but still small segment. Entering CN would need a 版号 (ISBN) licence and real-name/anti-addiction systems, even for mini-games [S41]. Treat China as **out of scope for a solo developer's first release**.

### 2b. Vietnam: large appetite, dominated by licensed Chinese imports

- Vietnam mobile games: **$825M revenue in 2025 (+9.16%), ~54M gamers**. Only ~7–10% of users pay. 73% of VN studios use hybrid monetisation [S42][S43].
- **81% of licensed games in Vietnam are of Chinese origin; 14% are domestic.** Vietnamese companies "mainly act as publishers rather than producers" [S44-VN, VnExpress, 28 Nov 2024].
- Kiếm hiệp/tiên hiệp MMORPGs are licensed from China and localised by Gamota (Tru Tiên 3D, Tiên Duyên Kiếm), Funtap (Tình Kiếm 3D, Nhất Kiếm Giang Hồ) and VNG (a Thiên Long Bát Bộ title) [S45-VN]. Most are older MMORPG licences. No reliable 2025 revenue split for tiên hiệp was found.
- Legend of Mushroom (a Chinese idle RPG with a whimsical cultivation-adjacent feel): >$11M in SEA by Aug 2024, with **Vietnam and Thailand each >25%** [S46].
- 2025 Vietnam revenue leaders: Last War and the local RPG "Big Bang Thời Không" [S47].

**Reading.** Vietnamese players understand and like tiên hiệp. However, the local grossing charts are dominated by MMORPG and idle-RPG imports with heavy IAP and large UA budgets. A solo **action** title will not beat them on their own terms. Vietnam is best used as a **soft-launch and community market** (cheap CPI, strong cultural fit), not the main revenue market.

### 2c. Western and global appetite

- Black Myth: Wukong: **25M+ copies** by Jan 2025 [S48]. It raised global awareness of Chinese mythology, but it is a $70M-class project.
- Tale of Immortal (鬼谷八荒): peak **~172k concurrent players** at Early Access (2021) [S49], mostly Chinese-speaking.
- Wandering Sword (wuxia RPG): ~200k copies in its first week (Sep 2023) [S50].
- Cultivation Story: Reincarnation (xianxia top-down action roguelike): "Very Positive", 92% of ~2k reviews [S51]. Small scale.
- "Chronicle of Wuxia & Xianxia Survivors" (upcoming Steam) [S52]. The xianxia survivor-like slot is starting to fill up.

**Reading.** Western players buy Chinese-myth action when **combat quality and art carry the game** (Wukong, Warm Snow). Xianxia terminology alone does not sell it. PC/Steam is the platform where premium xianxia action is proven. Premium mobile is a secondary channel.

## 3. Market gap: where a small team can compete

| Candidate niche | Evidence it sells | Competition | Solo + AI feasibility (9–15 months) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gacha anime action ARPG | Very strong (§1a) | Teams of 500+ | ✗ | Avoid |
| Idle / sim cultivation (寻道大千-like) | Very strong in CN, strong in VN | Fierce. 37 Interactive, Joy Net and others buy UA at scale | Technically feasible, but it is **not TTK's direct-control identity** and it is UA-bound | Only as a meta layer |
| **Xianxia action roguelite, run-based, direct control, cultivation meta** | Adjacent proof: Warm Snow, Soul Knight, Grimvalor, Otherworld Legends (partial), Cultivation Story | Low–moderate on mobile. Moderate on Steam | ✓ with tight scope: 1 hero, 3–4 biomes/arenas, bosses, build system | **Best fit** |
| **Xianxia survivor-like (auto-attack + dodge/parry skills)** | Very strong genre proof (Brotato, VS, 20MTD, Magic Survival, Survivor.io) | High and growing (new xianxia survivors on Steam) | ✓✓ Easiest scope | Viable but crowded. Needs a sharp hook |
| Hybrid-casual Archero-like xianxia | Proven template (Archero 2, 我功夫特牛) | Very high (Habby and CN publishers) | Build ✓, UA ✗ without a publisher | Only with a publisher partner |

**The gap TTK can plausibly own:** *short-session (5–15 min) arena action, where skill expression comes from perfect-parry and elemental combos, wrapped in cultivation-realm meta-progression.* Checking against the sources:

- Survivor-likes remove direct control, so a parry-centric design stands out.
- Chinese cultivation hits lack skill combat.
- Premium action roguelites (Grimvalor, Warm Snow) lack a persistent cultivation fantasy.

Risk: this gap may exist because the combination is hard to sell in store screenshots and short videos. Creative testing must confirm the hook before full production.

## 4. Monetisation models and benchmarks

### 4a. Model fit for a solo developer

| Model | Proof | Pros for TTK | Cons / risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Premium ($4.99–7.99) | Pascal's Wager 1M+, Warm Snow mobile, Grimvalor | No LiveOps; no gacha law exposure; fits "complete product" | Low mobile discovery; piracy; revenue concentrated at launch |
| **Free trial + premium unlock** | Grimvalor Android [S28] | Removes the price barrier; one IAP; honest | Needs a strong first 20 minutes |
| Ads only / ads + one IAP | Vampire Survivors mobile [S22][S23]; 我功夫特牛 | Maximises reach in VN/SEA; low ARPDAU is acceptable at low CPI | Needs large DAU; ads can hurt the premium feel |
| Hybrid-casual IAP + IAA | Archero 2, Survivor.io, 寻道大千 | Highest ceiling | Needs a UA budget, LiveOps, economy design. Blended ARPDAU of hybrid-casual is ~$0.15–0.50 [S53] (weak secondary source) |
| Battle pass (cosmetic) | Common in mid-core | Recurring revenue without randomness | Needs a content cadence a solo developer may not sustain |
| Gacha | Top-grossing category | — | **Not recommended**: see compliance below |

### 4b. Regulatory notes on randomised monetisation

- **Vietnam (Decree 147/2024, in force 25 Dec 2024).**
  - Games are classified G1–G4. G1 (multiplayer on an enterprise-hosted server) needs a licence plus a publishing decision. G2–G4 need a certificate and a notification confirmation [S54][S55].
  - Virtual items may not be exchanged for cash or traded between players [S56].
  - App stores must remove unlicensed games on request [S55].
  - **Implication:** even a single-player IAP game sold to Vietnamese users likely needs a registered enterprise and a G2/G4 confirmation. Get legal advice.
- **South Korea.** Probability disclosure for loot boxes has been mandatory since **22 Mar 2024** (in the UI, on the website and in ads). 266 games were found violating it in the first months, 60% of them foreign [S57][S58].
- **China.** A 版号 (ISBN) licence is required. Minors are limited to 3 hours per week and spending caps apply [S41][S59].
- **EU.** The CPC Network's key principles on virtual currencies (21 Mar 2025) are soft law: show real-money prices and avoid hidden costs [S60].
- **Japan.** The "kompu gacha" ban dates from 2012. This is general knowledge and was not re-sourced this session.

- **Top-quartile target:** D1 ≥ 30%, D7 ≥ 8–10%, median session ≥ 8 min.
- **Kill or rework:** D1 < 18% (below the Action median).

## 5. Positioning options for TTK

### Option A (recommended): "Xianxia Arena Roguelite" — premium-unlock, mobile + Steam

- **Pitch:** Enter a series of sealed arenas as a young cultivator. Parry demon strikes, chain lightning/wind elements, break through a realm after each boss. 10–15 minute runs, persistent cultivation tree.
- **Audience:** mid-core action-roguelite players (Soul Knight, Warm Snow, Hades, Grimvalor); anime/xianxia fans in VN/SEA/CN-diaspora; Steam Chinese-myth action buyers.
- **Comparables:** Warm Snow ($5M in first month on Steam) [S30], Grimvalor [S28], Pascal's Wager [S29], Cultivation Story [S51].
- **Scope (9–15 months, solo + AI):**
  - 1 playable hero with 3 elemental schools.
  - ~12 arena layouts across 3 regions, ~20 enemy types, 5–6 bosses.
  - 40–60 build modifiers.
  - Meta: cultivation realms of 5 tiers.
  - No online services.
- **Business model:**
  - Free trial of the first region + one-time unlock at $4.99–6.99, or regional pricing around ~₫99k–149k in VN.
  - Optional cosmetic DLC later.
  - Steam version at $9.99–14.99 with a demo to collect wishlists.
- **Why:** It fits TTK's direct-control/parry identity, avoids gacha rules and LiveOps, and a single-player G4-style release is the lightest licensing path.
- **Risks:**
  - Low discoverability of premium games on mobile.
  - Combat quality has to reach Warm Snow/Grimvalor level (animation and feel are the bottleneck for a solo developer).
  - Piracy on Android.
  - The Otherworld Legends case shows combo action can underperform on other platforms.

### Option B: "Xianxia Survivors+" — F2P, ads + light IAP, VN/SEA-first

- **Pitch:** A survivor-like with **one active parry/dash button** and elemental skill evolution. 5–8 minute stages; cultivation realms work as account progression.
- **Audience:** mass mobile players in VN/SEA/LATAM (Vampire Survivors, Magic Survival, Survivor.io players).
- **Comparables:** Magic Survival [S24], Vampire Survivors mobile [S22], Survivor.io [S20], 20MTD [S32].
- **Scope:** the smallest content-to-hours ratio. Systemic content (weapons × elements) is well suited to AI-assisted production.
- **Business model:**
  - Rewarded ads (revive, double loot).
  - One "Immortal Supporter" pack that removes forced ads and adds cosmetics.
  - Later a cosmetic season pass.
  - No gacha.
- **Risks:**
  - A crowded genre, with xianxia survivor clones already appearing [S52].
  - Ads-based revenue needs hundreds of thousands of DAU.
  - Weak differentiation from TTK's "direct-control" vision if auto-attack takes over.
  - UA costs rising in SEA [S63].

### Option C: "Hybrid-casual xianxia action" with a publisher

- **Pitch:** Archero-style room-clearing with direct movement and skill-button casting. Chapter-based, 3–5 minute rooms. Deeper cultivation, equipment and pet meta.
- **Audience:** hybrid-casual RPG players; Tier-1 plus SEA.
- **Comparables:** Archero 2 [S17–S19], 我功夫特牛 [S34], 寻道大千 hybrid economy [S35][S36].
- **Scope:** the core game is feasible. The economy, LiveOps, analytics and UA creative loop are not feasible alone. A **publisher partner is required** (e.g. a VN publisher such as Gamota/Funtap/VNG, or a CN hybrid-casual publisher).
- **Business model:** IAP + IAA, with a publisher revenue share of ~50–70% to the publisher (typical; not sourced this session).
- **Risks:**
  - Dependence on the publisher.
  - Pressure toward pay-to-win.
  - Habby-level competition, where Archero 2 earns over $11 lifetime revenue per download [S19].
  - Loss of creative control.

**Recommendation:**

1. Pursue **Option A** as the product spine.
2. Design the combat so a lighter **Option B-style "Endless Trial" mode** can be added. This gives a free, ad-supported entry point in VN/SEA if premium sales stall.
3. Validate early:
   - a 60-second parry/elemental gameplay video;
   - a Steam page plus a demo for wishlists;
   - a VN/SEA TestFlight or closed Android test measured against the §4c gates.

## 6. Uncertainty notes

1. **Revenue estimates** (Sensor Tower, AppMagic) are modelled, exclude Chinese Android, and often disagree. For example, Survivor.io is reported at $476M in one source and >$500M in another.
2. **Team sizes** for most small studios (Direlight, LEME, BadMud, Veewo) were not confirmed in this session. "Solo" for Magic Survival is reported, not verified.
3. **No 2025 revenue split for tiên hiệp/kiếm hiệp in Vietnam** was found. Vietnamese sources mostly offer listicles rather than data. Statements about Vietnamese appetite rely on the share of Chinese imports [S44] and Legend of Mushroom's Vietnamese share [S46].
4. **Genre-level retention for Action** comes from one GameAnalytics dataset (D28, not D30). Public genre-level ARPDAU data for RPG/action does not exist; the hybrid-casual ARPDAU figure is from a weak secondary blog.
5. **The Decree 147 reading** (enterprise and certificate needed even for single-player IAP games) is from law-firm summaries. It is not legal advice; confirm with a Vietnamese counsel.
6. **我功夫特牛 data is from 2019**, outside the 2023–2026 window, and is used only as a precedent.
7. Not researched due to search budget: NGA/TapTap review mining, Snowbreak/Dislyte/Eternal Evolution financials, Shadow Fight 4 revenue, 觅长生 and 了不起的修仙模拟器 sales, and Japanese xianxia appetite beyond Wuthering Waves.
