# PLAYTEST_LOG.md

Append-only log. One entry per session: date · build (exact commit) · who played (Director / outside player, never personal data) · what they did · quotes · what we change.
Friday Director session: 60–90 min on a real phone. From G1 on, add outside players every two weeks.

Suggested questions after play:
- "Tell me that run as if telling a friend." Pass if they name a specific moment.
- What was your favourite moment? What frustrated you?
- Did you want another run?

## History before 2026-09-26 (summary)

These are condensed from the archived evidence in `docs/archive/governance-v2/evidence/`. All sessions were played by the Director alone.

- **2026-08-17 · P0A+ arena.** Wanted to keep playing: yes. Dodge→counter worked. The archetypes felt distinct. Water Shift and Spirit Wind events: "FUN". This was the strongest fun signal so far.
- **2026-08-18 · Vertical Slice v0.1.** The foundation was accepted, but the player-facing experience "still feels too demo-like".
- **2026-08-20 · Slices 001–003.** The effects were a "boring demo" (001). Slice 002: the gameplay foundation "ok if developed further", but the VFX barely changed. Slice 003: the VFX were not better.
- **2026-08-21 · Slices 004–006 (VFX techniques, hero VFX).** "No difference". Slice 006 was confounded by the greybox world, and the VFX axis was closed.
- **2026-08-28 · B-LITE (chibi sprites).** "Still the same".
- **2026-08-29 · Slice 009 (representative combat spine).** Human Product Gate NO: it reads as a demo rather than a market-facing game. Audio also NO.

Lesson recorded in ADR 004/005: the verdict was about presentation fidelity, measured again and again with one tester. From now on, lock the standards (G2) and bring in outside players.
