# ttk-onboarding-accessibility

## WHEN TO USE

Touching the first session, first 30–60 seconds, tutorialization, HUD readability, controls discoverability, contrast/text, motion/flash intensity, input alternatives, or accessibility-sensitive interaction.

## PRODUCT QUESTION

Can a new player perceive, reach and understand the essential interaction without prior project knowledge, while avoidable sensory/input barriers are kept out of the core experience?

## MUST

- Test first-session understanding with players who are not relying on developer/operator memory when the task claims onboarding quality.
- Keep essential information perceivable through more than color alone when practical; preserve object/threat clarity under combat motion.
- Respect touch target size, spacing, safe areas and comfortable reach on representative phones.
- Provide visible control feedback and avoid requiring unexplained simultaneous/precision gestures unless the game deliberately teaches them.
- Treat text legibility, contrast, motion/flash load, haptic intensity and time pressure as design constraints where they materially affect the slice.
- Use platform guidance as guardrails, not as a claim of legal/certification compliance.

## MUST NOT

- Defer all accessibility/readability work to a final compliance pass.
- Assume the Human/Game Director's familiarity proves onboarding.
- Use color-only threat/state communication when another cue is feasible.
- claim accessibility certification from following this skill.

## EXIT CONDITION

The active slice's onboarding/readability barriers are explicitly tested or recorded as out of scope. A representative Human gate distinguishes `new-player understandable` from `operator can play it because they already know how`.

## External basis

Apple game-control guidance emphasizes touch reach, safe areas, control size and visible/tactile states. Microsoft XAGs frame accessibility guidance as development guardrails covering text, contrast, input, object clarity, haptics, UI, motion and photosensitivity rather than end-only compliance.
