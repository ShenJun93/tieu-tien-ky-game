# ttk-enemy-ai-encounter-direction

## WHEN TO USE

Designing or changing enemy behavior, archetypes, telegraphs, group pressure, boss/elite pacing, spawn composition, or encounter logic.

## PRODUCT QUESTION

Does each enemy create a readable pressure problem with intentional counterplay, and do combined enemies produce authored combat decisions rather than undifferentiated chase-and-hit noise?

## MUST

- Give each required archetype a pressure role: e.g. pursuit, spacing denial, line threat, disruption, burst commitment, protection, or a task-specific alternative.
- Expose intent before consequence through motion/pose/telegraph/audio/spatial setup appropriate to the threat.
- Define player counterplay and recovery windows; difficulty without interpretable agency is not Readable Chaos.
- Test group composition, not only single-agent behavior. Pairings should create deliberate positional/timing problems rather than accidental dogpiles.
- Use arena routes, chokepoints, water/hazards and cultivation interactions when they materially serve the encounter promise.
- Keep authoritative damage/state in gameplay logic; telegraphs/presentation communicate it.

## MUST NOT

- Differentiate enemies only by HP/damage/speed/color.
- Treat deterministic AFK defeat as evidence the encounter is fun or readable.
- Add broad behavior-tree/GOAP infrastructure before concrete repeated needs justify it.
- Scale enemy count to manufacture intensity when readability is already failing.

## EXIT CONDITION

Automated tests cover mechanical invariants; representative play confirms threat intent, counterplay and group pressure are understandable in motion. Human acceptance is required for the claimed encounter FEEL/READS dimensions.
